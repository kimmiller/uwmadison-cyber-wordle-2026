How to play

  1. Guess the 5-letter cybersecurity-related word for the selected date in October 2026.
  2. After each guess, tiles will change color to show how close your guess was:
    • Green — Correct letter in the correct spot.
    • Yellow — Correct letter in the wrong spot.
    • Gray — Letter not in the word.
  3. You have 6 attempts to guess the word.
  4. Your wins for the month are tracked locally in your browser.
